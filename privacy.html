Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

liisa-clients is a private tool used only by its owner. It signs in with the owner's own Google account and reads Google Ads reporting data for accounts the owner already has access to.

What data it accesses

Google Ads account, campaign and performance data, through the Google Ads API, only for accounts the signed-in user is authorized to view.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce reports and analysis for the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Reporting data is not stored on any server operated by this tool.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/liisa-sk/liisa-sk.github.io.
